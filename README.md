# QuranFoundation source snapshots

Public source-content mirror containing original commentary response archives for resource IDs 14, 15, 157, 159, 160 and 818. Source JSON bytes and provenance are retained unchanged.

The two Arabic archives contain the captured chapter responses. Additional per-ayah recovery responses are still needed for 31 references in resource 14 and 40 in resource 15; this repository does not claim complete commentary coverage. The four Urdu archives include the captured grouped-response recovery requests and explicitly unavailable entries.

Hafs word-audio archives have been prepared; their publication and individual-file delivery are in progress. A repository being public does not mean every draft asset is downloadable or integrated into an app.

Original publisher and documentation: https://quran.com/ and https://api-docs.quran.com/ . Source credits and existing rights remain with their respective owners. Redistribution confirmation remains pending; publication is not a claim of ownership or a new license.

Only source content and its provenance belong here. App implementation, tools, credentials and user data are excluded. Retrieval dates are not claimed as original publication dates.
